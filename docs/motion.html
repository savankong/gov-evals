Motion, in full

Every animation in the product, running on the production tokens. Two durations and two curves do all of the work. Nothing bounces, nothing loops except a live campaign, and everything here stops entirely when the operating system asks for reduced motion.

Reduced motion

Not a degraded path. A supported one.

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce

Under the OS setting, every animation and transition on this page collapses to 0.01ms, the shimmer is removed outright, and figures are assigned their final value without counting. The interface is identical; it simply arrives. Turn the setting on and reload to check — nothing on this page should move.
